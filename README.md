# Inside 24h

**See what happens inside your body in the 24 hours after you eat or drink something.**

A 3D simulation of 149 foods and drinks — where they go, how long they stay, what enters your blood, and what they do to your sugar, hunger, sleep and hydration. Every claim is graded by evidence and linked to its source.
